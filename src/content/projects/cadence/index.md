Cadence is an iPhone app for [intervals.icu](https://intervals.icu). It talks to your intervals.icu account directly from your phone, with no account of its own and no server in between. It's in beta on TestFlight.

## What it does

**Today and the calendar**
- Your week at a glance, with planned sessions, races, notes and days off
- One editor for every calendar entry, with a step-by-step workout builder
- Race pages with the course map, climb and elevation profile from shared races

**Activities**
- A hub per activity: Timeline, Pace, Heart rate, Power, Route and Laps, in intervals.icu's own order
- Curves against your last 42 days, and zones with the same numbers intervals.icu shows

**Insights and profile**
- Fitness, fatigue and form, plus widgets for your board
- Sports, zones, availability and gear with wear and service reminders
